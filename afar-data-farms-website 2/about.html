Our story

A practical bridge between agriculture and information.

Afar Data Farms is built around the idea that agricultural knowledge has lasting value when it is preserved, understood and made useful.

We bring together agriculture, data and education to help farmers, agribusinesses and communities learn from reliable information and make more informed choices.

Our work is grounded in stewardship, integrity, purposeful innovation and community empowerment.

Purpose & direction

What guides our work.
